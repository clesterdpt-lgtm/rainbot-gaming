Raid The Base

Spend Hype to send squads through three defended lanes. Crack two lab shields, overwhelm the lab, free the alien, and reach the van before lockdown hits 100%.

Click a deploy card, then click/tap a lane to send it. Keyboard: 1-4 squads, ZXC lanes, Q deploys, E rushes, P pauses.

    Raid notes
    • This is reverse tower defense: you send units through lanes while the base tries to stop them.
    • Click a deploy card to choose a squad. The selected card says SELECTED, then click a lane to send it.
    • Runners are fast, Sign Guys tank, Livestreamers generate Hype, and Tinfoil Crew jams nearby defenses.
    • Break the pink Lab Shield gate in two different lanes before the lab core can take damage.
    • Once the lab opens, escort the alien to the van before lockdown reaches 100%.
    • Enemy defenses are labeled on the map. Sirens raise lockdown fastest, drones punish clusters, lights soften lanes, and guards shoot front units.
