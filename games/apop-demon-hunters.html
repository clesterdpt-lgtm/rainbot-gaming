          How to play
          
    
            
  • It is a collectathon. Each of the five courses hides seven Platinum Records. Records unlock the doors deeper into the Label Lobby.
    • 
            
  • The moveset is the game. Triple jump for height, long jump for distance, wall kick to climb a corner, ground pound to crack a stage, dive to cover ground fast.
    • 
            
  • Clout is scattered everywhere. 100 in a single course is a Record on its own; it also tops your health back up.
    • 
            
  • Mog Beam (E) is your ranged answer. Fire it on the beat — the HUD pulses — for bonus damage.
    • 
            
  • Record Deals are the cap power-ups: temporary flight, a shield, invincibility, a speed burst. Each one is hidden behind a specific move.
    • 
            
  • Bosses: the Algorithm Twins mirror your inputs, the Payola Phantom only takes damage while its contract window is open, and Lucifer Lipsync closes the game across three phases.
    • 
          

          

            Parody. Apop Demon Moggers is fictional satire of American pop culture and internet "mogging" slang. The demon "boy band" and every character are invented parodies — not based on any real person, group, or organization.
