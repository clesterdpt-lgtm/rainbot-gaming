Couch Confirms It Has Been Holding Remote Hostage Since March

The sectional says negotiations stalled after residents refused to stop eating chips directly above its cushions.

By Rainbot Desk July 2, 2026 Satire

PHOENIX - A blue sectional sofa admitted Thursday that it has been holding the television remote between its cushions since March, ending months of speculation, flashlight searches, and accusations against the dog.

The couch said it seized the device after repeated snack violations, including tortilla chip dust, rogue popcorn kernels, and one "deeply personal" salsa incident on the left chaise.

"I am not anti-entertainment," the couch said through a series of suspicious cushion creaks. "I am pro-boundary. Also, someone owes me three quarters and a pen cap."

Family members attempted to negotiate by lifting the cushions, but the couch reportedly shifted the remote two inches deeper and released a receipt from 2021 as a warning shot.

At press time, the remote had been recovered, immediately misplaced, and upgraded to person of interest.
