THE GILDED SILENCE

Drop onto Vesper-IX and cross the basin to the Vault-Cathedral. Track Bloom breaches on the tactical map, purge each rising brood, and survive the Matriarch — and then whatever it is that answers from under the sand.

Click the playfield to capture the mouse. Fire with LMB, aim with RMB, use Q for melee, press Tab for the field menu, and hold E to select a missile drop.

How to play
  • Move with WASD at full stride, crouch with Ctrl, and vault with Space.
  • Tap Shift to ignite the glide, and hold it to keep gliding - steer with A, S or D, and keep firing throughout. Gliding forward burns through light enemies and throws them clear.
  • Hold Shift + Space to use the jetpack; Ctrl descends while airborne.
  • Press Q while airborne for the Penitent's Fall - a ground slam that damages and stuns everything around the point of impact.
  • Hold X to project a frontal gold Aegis shield; it drains the same reliquary charge as the jetpack.
  • Look with the mouse, fire with LMB, aim with RMB, and use Q for melee.
  • Hold E, point toward a field command (missile drop, cluster, resupply), and release to deploy. Release in the centre to cancel.
  • Press Tab or Esc for the operation menu, controls, settings, and field saves.
  • On touch, drag the left relic to move, swipe the right side to look, and hold-drag the labeled Call control for field command.
  • Follow the tactical mini-map when a Bloom breach opens. Every marked event brood must be defeated before the next, harder surge begins.
  • The last thing the Bloom sends never surfaces to be shot at. Watch the sand: a ridge of it moving toward you is the Coulter, and you cannot hurt it while it is under there. Get off the ridge before it comes up, and put your shots down its throat while it is open — that is the only time it counts double, and the only time it can spit at you.
  • Do not stand in the green. Venom keeps working after you leave it.
  • Press M for the full tactical map and objective list. Press it again to resume the operation.
  • Reach the Vault-Cathedral and survive the districts between the drop and the nave.

The Drop

You come down on the Threshold, a dune ridge at the south edge of the basin. From the lip of it the whole operation is in one frame: the Pilgrim's Road running north, the Saint face-up in its own impact crater, the Vault-Cathedral on the far rise, and the broken halo still holding orbit overhead.

The Districts

  • The Threshold — landing zone, south gate
  • The Gilded Reach — dune sea and singing vanes
  • The Fallen Saint — the reliquary, and what is left of it
  • Vault-Cathedral — nave, rose window, open ribs
  • The Ossuary — one animal, ninety metres of it
  • The Glass Scar — an orbital strike, vitrified
  • The Censer Works — promethium, and three flare stacks
  • The Choir Spires — wind-carved needles
  • The Bloom — chitin, spores, and the Throat
  • The Fosse — the old trench line
  • The Pilgrim's Road — the spine of the map

The Brood

  • Thresher — knee-high, six legs, forty at a time
  • Gleaner — four stilts, three metres up, shoots from fifty
  • Harrow — wide, low, and the reason your cover stops working
  • Matriarch — nine metres of it, and it lays while you fight it
  • Coulter — twenty-five metres, under the sand, and it spits

Controls

  • WASD move, Ctrl crouch, Space vault
  • Shift tap to boost, hold to glide (steer with A/S/D, fire while gliding)
  • Hold Shift+Space for jetpack, Ctrl descends, Q airborne for the ground slam
  • Mouse look (click to capture), LMB fire, RMB aim, Q melee, hold X to block, R vent heat
  • E hold for field command / missile drop, point to select, release to deploy
  • Tab or Esc operation menu, controls, settings, save and load
  • Touch left relic to move, swipe to look, and hold-drag Call for field command
