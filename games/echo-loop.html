⭯ ECHO LOOP

Press R to close a loop: the room rewinds and a translucent echo replays your last run forever. Stand on your echoes, make them hold doors, let them push crates. A few past selves, one puzzle.

Move with A/D or arrows, Space jumps. R closes the loop (records an echo), Z retries the current loop, U undoes an echo, P pauses.

FIELD GUIDE

🧍You (live) — hot pink, being recorded. Always.
👻Echo — cyan ghost of a past run. Stand on it. Ride it. It replays exactly, every loop.
📦Crate — pushable, presses plates. Rewinds each loop, but your echo re-pushes it.
▬Plate & door — matching numbers. Door stays open only while the plate is held.
⏱Timed gate — yellow bars on the loop's own schedule. Open only during its window, every loop. Waiting is a move.
┄One-way deck — jump up through it, land on top of it.
▲Spikes — a paradox. Loop rewinds, echoes survive.
How to play
  • Everything you do is recorded. Press R to close the loop: the room rewinds — crates, doors, you — and your last run replays as a translucent echo, on repeat, forever.
  • Echoes are solid (on top). Land on an echo's head and stand there. Stack echoes into a staircase, or park them over a pit as stepping stones.
  • Echoes do chores. An echo standing on a pressure plate holds its door open. An echo that pushed a crate re-pushes it every loop, at the same moment.
  • Loop budget. Each room caps how many simultaneous selves you get (2–5). Fewer loops and faster clears score more. Z retries the current run without spending a slot.
  • The timer is the loop. When it hits zero the room rewinds and your uncommitted run is lost — close the loop before then if you want to keep it.
  • Chrono Shards. Each level cleared earns a shard: one free echo undo. Out of shards? A quick ad mints a new one (Pro loopers undo free).
