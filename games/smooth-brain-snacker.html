🧠 SMOOTH BRAIN SNACKER

You're a brain worm. Slither around and gobble up smart, responsible ideas — then watch them come out the other end as glorious brainrot. Every idea you digest makes your trail of terrible takes longer.

Don't hit the walls. Don't eat your own brainrot.

Steer with ←↑↓→ / WASD, swipe, or the arrows in the panel. P pauses.

    How to play
    • Eat the glowing idea. Its name flashes overhead when it appears. Digest it for points, grow a segment, and drop the brainrot it became as a 💩 behind you.
    • Grab the 🧠 Big Brain idea when it pulses in. Big bonus points, but it ghosts away if you dawdle.
    • Mind your tail. The longer your brainrot trail, the easier it is to crash into your own bad takes.
    • Walls are fatal. No wrap-around — that's the edge of your attention span.

    Parody. A satirical sendup of "brainrot" internet culture. Slither-and-grow gameplay inspired by the classic Snake. Not affiliated with anyone. Go touch grass afterward.
