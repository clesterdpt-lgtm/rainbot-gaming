⭯ ECHO LOOP 3D

The looping room, from the inside. WASD moves, mouse looks, Space jumps. R closes the loop — a translucent echo replays your last run forever. Hold Shift to fast-forward only your echoes. Climb your own shoulders. Leave yourself holding the door.

Left half of screen: move stick · Right half: look

Enter the loop captures the mouse. Esc or P opens the game menu; Resume re-captures it. WASD move, Space jumps, R closes the loop (records an echo), hold Shift to fast-forward echoes 5×, Z retries the current loop, and U undoes an echo.

LOOP CONTROLS

FIELD GUIDE

👁You (live) — first person, being recorded. Always.
👻Echo — glowing cyan ghost of a past run. Land on its head, ride it, climb it.
📦Crate — pushable, presses plates. Rewinds each loop, but your echo re-pushes it.
▬Plate & door — matching numbers and colors. Door stays open only while the plate is held.
◉Watcher — a red security eye that locks onto the first live or echo body it sees. Jump, take cover, or send a decoy.
━Sweeper — a low moving light bar with a real hit volume. Jump it or rewind the current run.
▼The void — falling is a paradox. Loop rewinds, echoes survive.
How to play
  • Everything you do is recorded. Press R to close the loop: the room rewinds — crates, doors, you — and your last run replays as a glowing echo, on repeat, forever.
  • Echoes are platforms. Land on an echo's head and stand there. Your jump clears a body height, so a stack of past selves is a staircase.
  • Echoes do chores. An echo on a pressure plate holds its door open. An echo that pushed a crate re-pushes it every loop, at the same moment.
  • Loop budget. Each room caps simultaneous selves (2–5). Fewer loops and faster clears score more. Z retries without spending a slot.
  • The timer is the loop. Zero means rewind — commit with R before it dies (the moonlight turns red as a warning).
  • Skip the waiting. Hold Shift to advance only your past echoes at 5× speed. You and the loop timer stay at normal speed.
  • Security begins on level 5. Watchers can be baited by echoes; low Sweepers must be jumped. A hit rewinds the live run but never deletes prior echoes.
  • Chrono Shards. Shared with 2D Echo Loop: each level cleared earns one free echo undo; a quick ad mints more (Pro loopers undo free).
