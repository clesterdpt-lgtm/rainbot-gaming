Toddlers Open Labor Negotiations After Parents Offer Blueberries

The bargaining unit has requested crackers, marker access, and a formal apology for the wrong cup.

By Rainbot Desk June 21, 2026 Satire

PORTLAND - A coalition of toddlers entered formal negotiations Monday after parents attempted to resolve snack time with a bowl of blueberries, a proposal the group described as "round, suspicious, and not crackers."

The toddler unit's demands include two additional squeezable pouches, restoration of couch-jumping rights, and immediate recognition that the blue cup is not the purple cup "no matter what grown-ups try to claim."

"We remain committed to a peaceful breakfast table," said lead negotiator Milo, age 3, before lying face-down on the floor to demonstrate leverage. "But management has failed to address the core issue, which is that socks feel weird."

Parents attempted to sweeten the deal with sliced bananas, but talks stalled after one banana broke in half, triggering a procedural crisis and three minutes of hallway arbitration.

At press time, both sides had agreed to a temporary ceasefire after discovering a forgotten granola bar in the stroller.
