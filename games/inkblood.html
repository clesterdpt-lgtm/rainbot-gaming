NIGHT PARADE

Move with WASD, arrow keys, or touch. Space/Shift performs an Ink Step; Q releases Blood Eclipse when its seal is full.

Open Menu or press Escape whenever you want to maximize the frame or review the controls.

How to play
  • Choose your slayer, then survive the fifteen-minute Night Parade.
  • Move to evade yokai; your equipped weapons attack automatically.
  • Dodge with Space or Shift. Ink Step has brief invulnerability and a short cooldown.
  • Kills fill the Blood Eclipse seal. Press Q when it turns crimson to cut every nearby yokai.
  • Collect ink shards, level up, and build a stronger loadout.
  • Press Escape or Menu to maximize the frame, review controls, or return to the page.
