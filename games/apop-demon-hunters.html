K-pop had demon hunters. America has demon MOGGERS.

Play Moggadonna's frontwoman: clear five neon stages, dodge spotlight traps, and blast a demon boy band off the charts — then disband Boyz II Hell and out-mog Lucifer Lipsync.

POWER-UPS

How to play
  • Run with ←/→ (or A/D). Jump with Space — tap again mid-air for a double jump.
  • Aim up with ↑/W to blast flying demons; crouch with ↓/S to duck under projectiles. On touch, the left stick does all four.
  • Press E to fire your Mog Beam. Fire when the beat dot flashes green for bonus damage.
  • Mog demons to build your MOG meter. When it's full, press F for a screen-clearing Mog Aura.
  • Reach the EXIT flag to clear stages 1–4. Stage 5 is the boss: Lucifer Lipsync (3 phases).
  • Power-ups (ad): Crowd Surf (6s invincible), Power Note (2× damage 8s), Encore Blast (wipe demons on screen).

Parody. Apop Demon Moggers is fictional satire of American pop culture and internet "mogging" slang. The demon "boy band" and every character are invented parodies — not based on any real person, group, or organization.
