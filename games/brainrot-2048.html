🧠 BRAINROT 2048

Slide the grid to smash matching memes together. Two NPCs fuse into a Skibidi, two Skibidis into Rizz… climb all the way to Galaxy Brain before the board clogs up.

Swipe, press ←↑↓→ / WASD, or use the arrows in the panel.

CONTROLS

TIER LADDER

    How to play
    • Slide every tile one direction (swipe, arrow keys, or WASD).
    • Matching tiles merge into the next tier up. A new NPC pops in after each move.
    • Keep space free. When the grid is full and nothing can merge, your brain is full — game over.
    • Goal: reach Galaxy Brain, the top of the food chain.
