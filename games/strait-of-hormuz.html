⛴ READY?

You're the SST Patience — a 2-million-barrel VLCC held together by gaffer tape and prayers. Sector 1 starts with mines; later sectors unlock nastier hazards, then boss walls.

POWER-UPS

How to play
  • Steer with A/D or ←/→ — hold for continuous turning.
  • Throttle with W/↑, brake with S/↓.
  • Horn (Space) clears nearby hazards and forces boss ships to move.
  • Take 3 hits and you explode. Share the wipeout card.
  • Survive sectors that unlock one new hazard family at a time.
  • Ad walls later in each sector offer temporary upgrades.
