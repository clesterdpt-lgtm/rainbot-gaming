America fought back. The demon elite didn't see it coming.

You play as The Diva. Sync your mic swings to the beat. Slay your way up Madison Ave, into a hedge-fund penthouse, through a private-equity nightmare dimension.

POWER-UPS

How to play
  • Move between 4 lanes with ↑/↓ or W/S.
  • Press Z to swing your mic. When the beat circle pulses green, hit Z for 2× damage.
  • Build a combo by chaining hits. X drops a nuke (5 combo, damages all elites).
  • Survive 3 stages. Stage 3 is boss vs Lavish Larry (3 phases).
  • Power-ups (ad): Crowd Surf (5s inv), Power Note (2× dmg 8s), Smash Hit (30 dmg all).
