    HOW TO PLAY

    • Earn by day. Stand near people and tap ACT to perform for tips. Keep performing — curious passers-by wander over, and bigger crowds pay better.
    • Chase the daily favor. Every dawn deals a different District Favor from a rotating deck — pack a park crowd, slip cops on peels, chain an act streak. Complete it for a reward before nightfall.
    • Work street gigs. Three regulars — Prophet Dave (Underpass), Granny Boombox (Camp Row), and Mascot Marv (Crosswalk Circus) — hang out under GIG! markers. Tap ACT next to one to take their job, do the thing, then return to hand it in for unique rewards.
    • Collect & equip props. Grab cones, a boombox, a plunger and more, open the BAG, tap an item, then tap the slot (1-4) where you want it. The equipped item changes what ACT and ATTACK do (a cone earns tips as a hat, or throws as a weapon).
    • Study the block. Tap MAP or the round minimap (or press M) to open the city map — the game pauses while you check districts, landmarks, and your current objective.
    • Shop the Pawn Cart. Tap ACT at Pete's cart to browse the full catalog — weapons (Mop Spear, Rubber Chicken, Trash Boombox), permanent run upgrades (faster sneakers, tip-boosting top hat, puffy jacket, cart caddy, hype man), and supplies. Watch for the DAWN DEAL — one item is 30% off each day.
    • Mind your stars. Aggressive moves near regular folks raise your wanted level faster when more NPCs see it. Hit 2 stars and the cops chase — lie low or rest at camp to cool down.
    • Survive the night. Tweeker Zombies swarm in — including Goo Spitters that lob slowing slime from range. Tap ATTACK to clear the wave, then dawn resets the block. Last 3 nights to win.

    CITY LOG

    Prototype notes
    • Move through a compact top-down city board with roads, pickups, cars, cops, and night enemies.
    • Two buttons: ACT earns money, ATTACK fights. Collected props in your bag flavor both.
    • Hitting bystanders or over-performing raises your wanted stars and brings the cops. Lie low or rest at camp to cool down.
    • At night, Tweeker Zombies spawn from alleys — clear the wave with whatever you've equipped.
    • The goal arrow tracks your active street gig or district favor by day, or the nearest zombie at night.

    Satire prototype. The target is absurd systems and slapstick city chaos, not real unhoused people or addiction.
