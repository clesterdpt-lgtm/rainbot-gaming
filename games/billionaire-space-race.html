🚀 BILLIONAIRE SPACE RACE

Pick your founder. Launch up past the apogee line — you have to actually reach space — then turn around, descend, and set down gently and upright on the ground pad. Travel from Earth to ocean droneships, a space station, the Moon, Mars, the asteroid belt, Venus, Titan, Europa, and (don't) the Sun. The pad keeps shrinking and moving. It's a metaphor. Also it's a rocket.

CONTROLS

How to play
  • Reach space first. Climb up past the dashed APOGEE line — the pad's beacons turn green once you've made it.
  • Then come back down and land on the ground pad: slow descent (vy), slow drift (vx), and nearly level. The gauges go green when you're safe.
  • Rotate with ← / → (or A / D) — tap, don't hold. Thrust with ↑ / Space / W, which pushes wherever the nose points.
  • Fuel is investor money. Run out mid-air and gravity collects.
  • Travel the system: Earth → ocean droneship → space station → Moon → Mars → asteroid belt → Venus → Titan → Europa → the Sun. Each world changes gravity, surface, and hazards; the pad shrinks and moves.
