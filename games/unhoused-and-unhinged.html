CITY LOG

Prototype notes
  • Move through a compact top-down city board with roads, districts, pickups, cars, cops, and night enemies.
  • Earn cash with daytime antics, scavenge food and water, and manage heat before nightfall.
  • High heat starts a chase. Use distance, camp recovery, and banana peels to cool it down.
  • At night, Tweeker Zombies spawn from alleys. Use the plunger, cones, peels, and stunts to clear the wave.
  • The current goal arrow points to food, water, busking, safety, supplies, or the nearest night threat.

Satire prototype. The target is absurd systems and slapstick city chaos, not real unhoused people or addiction.
