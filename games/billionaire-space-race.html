🚀 BILLIONAIRE SPACE RACE

You are a tech tycoon with too much rocket. Burn the investor money, climb past lawsuit drones and audit satellites, then set down gently and upright on the platform — which keeps shrinking and, eventually, moving. It's a metaphor. Also it's a rocket.

CONTROLS

How to play
  • Rotate with ← / → (or A / D). The rocket keeps spinning — tap, don't hold.
  • Thrust with ↑ / Space / W. Thrust pushes wherever the nose points.
  • Land on the green pad: slow descent (vy), slow drift (vx), and nearly level. The gauges go green when you're safe.
  • Fuel is investor money. Run out mid-air and gravity collects.
  • Each level the pad shrinks, then moves, and the airspace fills with hazards. Clear it to earn an ad-powered upgrade.
