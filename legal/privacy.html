Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-28

Rainbot Network ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what data we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Local game data: guest high scores, saves, gameplay time, profile stats, and power-up inventory may be stored in your browser via localStorage.
  • Account data: if you sign in, we store your email login, display name, profile details, cloud saves, and high scores through our backend provider.
  • Community content: forum posts, replies, game/video/article comments, comment votes, reports, display names, and leaderboard entries may be public.
  • Notify-me emails: if you sign up to be notified about a future game, we store your email. That's it.
  • Standard analytics (optional): if we enable analytics, we use privacy-friendly tools (Plausible / Umami) that do not track individuals.

Backend provider

When accounts are enabled, authentication, profiles, cloud saves, high scores, forum data, comments, and comment votes are processed by Supabase. Do not post private information in public community areas.

Ads

Free users see ads. We use third-party ad networks (e.g. Adsterra, Google AdSense). Those networks may set cookies or use device identifiers to serve relevant ads. Pro subscribers see no ads.

Children

Rainbot Network is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
