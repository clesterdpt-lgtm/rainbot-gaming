⛏️ RIZZ-CRAFT

You spawned in a full 3D Ohio voxel world. Chop Skibidi Logs, mine Ohio Stone, and dig for Rizz and Sigma Ore.

Build a Crafting Toilet, light Rizz Torches, and survive the night when the Skibidi Toilets come.

Loading world...

CONTROLS

Right-click to place blocks, or eat when holding food. Open Crafting (E) at a Crafting Toilet to build tools, lamps, and meals. In Creative, left-click breaks any block instantly and double-tap Space to fly (Space up, Shift down). Switching back to Survival restores your inventory.

AD BOOSTS

Pro players get these instantly. Everyone else watches the mock rewarded ad.

How to play
  • Punch trees for Skibidi Logs, then craft Toilet Planks & Ohio Sticks.
  • Build a Crafting Toilet and place it — right-click it or stand near it to craft tools.
  • Mine down with a pickaxe: Gyatt Coal → torches, Rizz Ore, deep Sigma Ore, and lava pockets far below.
  • Light Rizz Torches, Rizz Lamps & Sigma Lanterns to keep the dark (and the mobs) away.
  • Cook & eat. Forage Wheat from grass and Berries from leaves, then craft Rizzbread, Glowcap Stew, a Sigma Energy Drink, or the Gilded Rizz Apple — right-click to eat and heal. Some give buffs.
  • Survive the night. Skibidi Toilets and Grimace Shakes spawn in the dark — swing a sword to send them back to Ohio.
  • Endgame: forge the Sigma Blade from Sigma Gems for the big score.
