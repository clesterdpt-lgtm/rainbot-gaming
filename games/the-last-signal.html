    Field manual
    • Matter is hand-gathered: send workers to the green crystal fields (right-click a node). They carry loads back to your command structure.
    • Energy and Signal flow only through extractors. Claim a node with a unit first, then build one on a gold vent or violet fracture — destroyed extractors release their node for either side to reclaim.
    • Your command structure trains workers; your production structure trains combat units. Click a building, then use the train buttons. Right-click ground to set its rally point.
    • Buildings (except extractors) must be placed within uplink range of an existing structure. Extractors ignore uplink range, but must sit on a claimed, free node.
    • Turrets unlock after your production structure stands. Heavy units cost Signal — no fracture, no titans.
    • Each faction fights differently: Humans build cheap and repair fast, Robots self-repair near their grid and extract more, Aliens field expensive elites with regenerating shields.
    • Skirmish setup: face 1, 2, or 3 enemy commands, pick their AI doctrine (Easy / Normal / Hard), and choose Full reveal or Fog of war on the faction select screen. With multiple enemies it's a free-for-all — rival commands fight each other too.
    • Terrain: mountain ranges are impassable — units path around them through the passes. The smaller sand-colored rockslides can be attacked and destroyed to open new routes across the map.
    • Victory: destroy every enemy command structure. Defeat: lose your own. Enemy AIs gather, expand outward, raid your economy, and attack in escalating waves — expect the first probe early and bigger armies late.
