THE ASCENT

Kenosis is a mountain, not a basin. You land at the Basecamp gate with the whole climb in one frame, and the Via Sacra spirals anticlockwise past seven stations to the Cathedral of the Ninth Ascent on the 452m summit.

The vigil is armed. Both operatives now carry a complete doctrine of abilities, and a trial yard forty metres from basecamp answers with a bestiary cohort and three flying censer-kites. Clear it, or climb past it — the mountain does not insist.

The nine stations
  • The Basecamp — south gate, 0m. Where you land.
  • The Black Tarn — SW, 40m. A lake of black ice and drowned pilgrims.
  • The Avalanche Bowl — SE, 60m. Open snowfield under a loaded headwall.
  • The Glacier Tongue — NW, 95m. Seracs, crevasses and three moulins.
  • The Rime Forest — E, 140m. Petrified conifers armoured in wind-driven rime.
  • The Fumarole Steps — NE, 160m. The mountain is not dead.
  • The Frozen Cascade — N, 210m. A 130m waterfall caught mid-fall.
  • The Bell Terrace — W, 240m. The Carillon Priory and its nine frozen bells.
  • The Cathedral of the Ninth Ascent — the summit, 452m.
How to play
  • Move with WASD, vault with Space, and click the playfield to capture the mouse.
  • White Vigil — hold left click for the alternating crescent volley; right click (or F) chains quick blade strikes, alternating fists; E is the Vigil Step, a 12m translation on two recharging charges. Airborne, that same melee becomes the stoop: a 21m thrust along whatever the reticle is pointing at — level for a horizontal lance, down for a dive.
  • Bastion Penitent — left click (or F) swings the reliquary hammer; right click casts it (it fells flyers and returns); hold E for the tower shield — an unlimited frontal guard. The Censer pack cannot fly: Shift + Space is a jet-boosted leap that clears eighteen metres, and F in the air is the Penitent's Fall.
  • Tap Shift to ignite the glide and hold it to keep gliding; hold Shift + Space for the jetpack (White Vigil's Augur carries the deeper tank).
  • Deep snow is slower than wind slab, and glacier ice will not hold an edge. The mountain decides your speed.
  • Press M for the ascent map. Press Esc for the field menu.
  • Crevasses are real holes. So is the drop off the Bell Terrace.
