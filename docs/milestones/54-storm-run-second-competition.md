# Milestone 54: Storm Run Second Competition

## Status

Implemented — automated acceptance complete; user playtest pending

## Objective

Add the mansion's second sanctioned reality-show competition as a readable, non-backtracking checkpoint race across the existing grounds. Storm Run becomes eligible only after Feast Says is complete, then calls the player after ten active exploration minutes or immediately after the next newly earned major clue. The triggering clue remains earned, further investigation pauses, and the player has five active minutes to check in with Mr. Feast at the rear-terrace production set before hearing his rules and racing through twelve ordered breadcrumb checkpoints while three close lightning bolts briefly reveal him in deep shadow. A no-show eliminates the player.

## Scope

- One authoritative `STORM_RUN` tuning table and explicit event state machine.
- A ten-minute active-exploration timer beginning at Feast Says completion and pausing with existing game pause surfaces.
- An early call on the first newly earned major clue after Feast Says, preserving that discovery before later clue progression pauses.
- A rear-terrace film-production set in place of the old report sign: one `0.72`-scale long-lens cinema/broadcast rig with at least `2.25m` host clearance, two studio lights, and a boom microphone frame a waiting Mr. Feast without crowding him. Mr. Feast waits `3m` out from the actual back-door plane and faces back toward the door, then turns to the held player as the movement-locked rear-door briefing begins. The player must face him and use E or touch Interact to begin. That check-in leads to the audible `3–2–1–GO` and west-facing race release. Twelve ordered checkpoints are distributed across the formal garden, front grounds, east lawn, hedge-maze interior, rear grounds, and pool terrace. After checkpoint one the route continues north through the garden's verified east clearance, crosses its front edge, and uses a carriage-turn marker to reach the front drive without returning toward the start. Every active marker uses a tall blue beacon, a raised guide configured to remain visible from the previous checkpoint, and a dedicated collection chime, with no leg longer than 32 metres.
- A five-active-minute called-state deadline shown by the minimal timer strip. Existing blocking UI pauses it, incomplete Mr. Feast/contestant asset loading never consumes it, and expiry produces the recoverable `ELIMINATED` state.
- A real player race using existing movement, sprint, stamina, collision, and mobile controls.
- Mara and Juniper visibly running the authored course with their existing compatible source run motions reduced through the checked-in stationary-animation extractor. Their measured world speed must remain below the player's maximum sprint speed.
- Exactly three authored lightning scares matching the annotated property map: northwest trees from the north-garden/front-yard approach, northeast trees from the front-door crossing, and a close hedge-maze south-turn reveal. Scares are independent route events with bounded checkpoint-progress windows. Proximity persistently arms the event for its route window; Mr. Feast must then be inside the camera frustum, pass a three-dimensional `0.90` forward-facing dot, and have an unobstructed torso ray before its one-shot can fire. All three figures remain believable human scale; the first two lurk `1.3–2.0m` beside real authored tree trunks without intersecting them and use a wider flash-only local fill instead of giant models, while runners never blink out and the still-visible checkpoint glow briefly subdues itself. A dedicated scare sting and close-bolt profile use a leading-silence-trimmed `2.1x` recorded roll after `0.02s`, a deeper `0.16` rain-bus duck, a `1.35x` flash, and `1.55x` exterior light multiplier. The former three-layer broadband white-noise crack is removed so no static-like transient precedes the thunder. A close strike reached during a temporary mobile Web Audio interruption queues until the next trusted gesture and then plays exactly once. He disappears as soon as the flash goes dark and no later than `1.2s`. The flash fill remains shader-resident at zero intensity throughout the event so the first scare never changes the renderer's point-light topology and compiles a shader on the reveal frame.
- A minimal race status strip showing only event, checkpoint count, and elapsed time. Mr. Feast's speech carries only the briefing, countdown, `Run!`, and outcomes; glowing beacons and collection chimes provide all checkpoint guidance.
- A witnessed back-door ending when the player wins: Mr. Feast announces Mara's elimination on the rear terrace, Mara covers her face with both hands for one sad final line, and Juniper physically walks through the terrace door and central stairs to her Reading Room routine. Mr. Feast is continuously held visible on his authored mark while the ending is active. Stepping just inside the back door cannot reset the scene; Mara disappears and Mr. Feast resumes patrol only after the player is both at least sixteen metres from the finish and no longer viewing it.
- A localized north-maze phone-readability refinement that selects the nearer entrance lamp and an existing row-three wayfinding lamp inside the unchanged six-spot grounds budget. The first bend must measure at least `0.16` light exposure and the phone canvas at least `0.035` luminance without lifting the second apparition out of deep shadow.
- Save/load persistence for the eligibility countdown, exact called-state deadline, completed state, and Mara elimination. Briefing/running saves restore to the clean production call with a fresh five-minute check-in window.
- Deterministic diagnostics and QA controls through `render_game_to_text()` and `window.MrFeastFresh`.

## Out of scope

- Lightning strike hazards, physics debris, falling trees, mud slowdown, knockback, damage, or ragdolls.
- Dynamic course generation, branching routes, a rearranging maze, or navigation AI.
- New character generation, rerigging, animation authoring, or external asset services.
- The previously drafted Lights Out competition, Game 3, Mara's death scene, or elimination-dinner aftermath.

## Dependencies

- **Depends on:** Milestone 35 player sprint/stamina, Milestone 44 storm audio, Milestone 47 contestant cast, Milestone 48 contestant locomotion, Milestone 52 Feast Says
- **Blocks:** later investigation ceiling and Game 3 milestones

## State graph

| From | Event | To |
|---|---|---|
| `dormant` | Feast Says complete, then ten active minutes or next major clue | `called` |
| `called` | player interacts with Mr. Feast at the rear-terrace production set | `briefing` |
| `called` | five active check-in minutes expire | `failed` |
| `briefing` | countdown expires | `running` |
| `running` | player reaches checkpoint twelve before Mara | `completed` |
| `running` | Mara reaches checkpoint twelve before player | `failed` |
| `failed` | load or restart | restored save or fresh `dormant` |

## Exit condition

User completes Feast Says, explores for ten minutes or earns the next clue, then reaches the rear-terrace film set within the five-active-minute call window. The player sees the long-lens cinema/broadcast rig, two studio lights, boom microphone, and waiting host, faces and interacts with Mr. Feast, sees him light up only for the face-to-face rules and audible `3–2–1–GO`, and releases facing west into normal storm darkness. The player follows twelve mutually visible and audibly confirmed ordered yard checkpoints north through the garden and around the front without backtracking, sees Mr. Feast unmistakably appear at the northwest trees, northeast trees, and close maze turn only when facing each direction, hears a loud recorded thunder roll over briefly ducked rain without a static-like crack, watches him vanish with the darkness, and reaches the finish before Mara. Mr. Feast announces the elimination, Mara stays visibly upset long enough to deliver her final line, and Juniper walks home. Stepping through the nearby back door leaves Mr. Feast visibly staged; only moving at least sixteen metres away while no longer viewing the scene removes Mara and returns the host to patrol. Missing the production call instead produces the recoverable no-show `ELIMINATED` screen.
