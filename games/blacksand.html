DEPLOYMENT

Pick a faction and a spawn on the deployment map, then hit DEPLOY to take the mouse. Choose Max for full-screen play; Esc releases the mouse and pauses.

Controls
  • WASD move · Shift sprint · Ctrl crouch · Z prone · Space jump
  • Mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB aim down sights
  • R reload · 1/2/3 weapons · E enter/exit vehicle
  • Q spot the enemy you are looking at · G frag
  • M theatre map · Tab scoreboard · Esc pause and options
  • On a phone: left stick moves, right half of the screen looks, thumb buttons do the rest.
Reading the HUD
  • The crosshair gap is the weapon's real cone. If it is wide, you will miss.
  • Red arcs point at whoever is shooting you, and stay pointed as you turn.
  • Diamonds in the world are objectives; hollow means terrain is in the way.
  • The bar under a ticket count is the bleed timer for that side.
Conquest
  • Five objectives. Stand inside one to capture it.
  • Whoever holds fewer points bleeds tickets. At zero, they lose.
  • Owning a point lets your side spawn on it.
  • Twenty minutes. Most tickets at the bell wins.
