City Pothole Becomes Historic Landmark After Surviving Three Mayors

Tourists have begun taking photos beside the crater while residents ask if the gift shop can sell alignments.

By Rainbot Desk July 2, 2026 Satire

CLEVELAND - A downtown pothole was granted historic landmark status Thursday after surviving three mayors, two public works apps, and one optimistic bag of cold patch.

The crater, known locally as "The Big One By The Pharmacy," has become a minor tourist destination after navigation apps began describing it as a scenic delay with strong local character.

"We considered fixing it," said one city official, "but at this point the pothole has more institutional memory than the parking department."

Residents remain divided. Some want immediate repairs, while others argue the hole should be preserved as a teaching site for young tires and first-time apartment hunters.

At press time, the pothole had filed paperwork to become a sister city with another pothole near the airport.
