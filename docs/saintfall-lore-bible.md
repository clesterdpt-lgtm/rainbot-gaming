# SAINTFALL — concise canon

## The premise

Saints are heavily armed, future-era human champions sent where conventional
forces cannot survive. A **saintfall** is their orbital descent: one Saint,
one drop pod, one mission below.

On **Vesper-IX**, the insect organism called **the Bloom** has overrun the old
Concord basin. Its mobile castes are known collectively as **the Brood**. The
operation is **The Gilded Silence**: break the invasion, destroy the false Saint
in the Vault-Cathedral, and reclaim the planet.

## The ancient Saint

The enormous statue at the basin's center is an ancient Concord monument to an
unknown Saint. It was built on Vesper-IX centuries before the present mission.
The Bloom shattered the statue and the surrounding district during its
invasion. The monument did **not** fall from orbit and is not the player.

Its original subject, age, and purpose are deliberately uncertain. The people
who built it left more reverence than explanation.

## What remains vague

- Who authorizes the Saints and how many still exist.
- Whether sainthood is a military rank, a religious office, or both.
- Why the Bloom can grow the **Apostate**, an imitation of a Saint.
- What the broken orbital halo once did.
- Why the Concord basin was important enough to defend with a colossal statue.

These questions are atmosphere, not required exposition. The player receives
clear mission orders; the deeper history stays fragmentary.
