Last to leave wins

          
Mr Feast promises one million dollars to the final contestant in his mansion. Survive his competitions, watch the cameras, and investigate what happened to Contestant 13.

          
The show is always filming. Sabotage carefully—and do not let the host catch you in a restricted room.

        


        

          How to play
          
    
            
  • Explore between competitions and follow clues hidden across all three floors and the grounds.
    • 
            
  • Speak with contestants. Their routines and stories change as the show eliminates people.
    • 
            
  • Search cabinets and closets for tools, keys, hiding places, and a flashlight.
    • 
            
  • Use darkness, crouching, and hiding spots to avoid Mr Feast and hostile cameras.
    • 
            
  • Complete Feast Says, Storm Run, and Feast Hunt while balancing investigation, sabotage, and survival.
    •
