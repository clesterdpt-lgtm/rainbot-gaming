💼 QUIET QUITTING

You're a burned-out remote worker trying to survive the 9-to-5. Navigate the cubicle farm, collect paycheck cents, and avoid getting dragged into pointless syncs.

Grab a glowing $350/hr Consultant Badge to turn all managers into panicked bootlickers and bill them for severance!

Steer with WASD / ←↑↓→ or swipe. Press P to pause, M for sound.

CONTROLS

OFFICE ROGUES GALLERY

Micromanager: Directly tracks you down to ask for updates.
Calendar Blocker: Aims 4 tiles ahead to drop 30m invites.
Intern: Chaotic flanker using vector pincer tactics.
HR Compliance Bot: Paces breakroom; stalks if far away.

LIVE SLACK FEED

  • 👋 Welcome to the team! Remember our core value: Extreme Synergy.
How to play
  • Clear the Cubicles: Chomp all paycheck cents and coffee beans on the floor to finish the shift and earn a promotion.
  • Promoted to Consultant: Eat a flashing pink badge to reverse the power balance. Chasing down a manager awards 200, 400, 800, and 1600 pts in a single surge!
  • Breakroom Perks: Grab periodic spawns like 🍕 Free Pizza, ☕ Cold Brew, and 🪂 Golden Parachutes for massive bonus cash.
  • Warp Tunnels: Slip through the left and right server closet vents to quickly loop across the floor plan.

Parody. A satirical homage to corporate work culture and classic 1980 arcade maze games. No actual interns or micromanagers were harmed during development.
