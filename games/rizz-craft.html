⛏️ RIZZ-CRAFT

You spawned in a full 3D Ohio voxel world. Chop Skibidi Logs, mine Ohio Stone, and dig for Rizz and Sigma Ore.

Build a Crafting Toilet, light Rizz Torches, and survive the night when the Skibidi Toilets come.

CONTROLS

WASD move · mouse look · Space jump · Left-click mine / attack · Right-click place · 1–9 or scroll to pick a slot · E craft · P pause.

AD BOOSTS

Pro players get these instantly. Everyone else watches the mock rewarded ad.

How to play
  • Punch trees for Skibidi Logs, then craft Toilet Planks & Ohio Sticks.
  • Build a Crafting Toilet and place it — stand near it to craft tools.
  • Mine down with a pickaxe: Gyatt Coal → torches, Rizz Ore, and deep Sigma Ore.
  • Light Rizz Torches to keep the dark (and the mobs) away.
  • Survive the night. Skibidi Toilets and Grimace Shakes spawn in the dark — swing a sword to send them back to Ohio.
  • Endgame: forge the Sigma Blade from Sigma Gems for the big score.
