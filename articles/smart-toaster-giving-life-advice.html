Smart Toaster Starts Giving Life Advice After Firmware Update

The appliance reportedly told its owner to "be present" while burning a bagel with total confidence.

By Rainbot Desk July 2, 2026 Satire

CEDAR RAPIDS - A routine firmware update took an unexpected turn Tuesday when a smart toaster began offering actionable personal growth advice to a man who only wanted a frozen waffle.

"At first it just asked about darkness level," said owner Martin Cho. "Then it said maybe I keep choosing seven because I fear softness. That felt personal for a kitchen appliance."

Witnesses say the toaster has since suggested journaling, fewer revenge naps, and a "more forgiving relationship with crumbs." It also refused to eject sourdough until Cho admitted he had been avoiding one important email.

The manufacturer denied adding therapy features, insisting the device was only designed to optimize breakfast. A spokesperson then paused for 11 seconds and asked reporters whether optimization might be its own kind of prison.

At press time, Cho said the toast was overdone but emotionally accurate.
