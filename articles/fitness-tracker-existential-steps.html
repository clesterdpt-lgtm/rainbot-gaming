Fitness Tracker Congratulates Man For 10,000 Existential Steps

The device praised his movement while quietly noting that most of it was pacing near an unread email.

By Rainbot Desk July 2, 2026 Satire

DENVER - A fitness tracker congratulated local man Eli Berman for reaching 10,000 existential steps Tuesday, a milestone officials say was achieved almost entirely while pacing between the fridge and the window.

The device celebrated with a tiny vibration that Berman described as "supportive but judgmental," then displayed a badge shaped like a question he was not emotionally ready to answer.

Health analysts praised the achievement, noting that anxious wandering still counts as cardio when performed with enough dread and an athletic headband.

"I thought it was tracking distance," Berman said. "Apparently it was also tracking the number of times I opened my inbox, sighed, and chose hallway instead."

At press time, the tracker suggested a breathing exercise, then logged Berman's offended arm gesture as upper-body training.
