TO THE MOON

Your trading rig crash-landed deep in a procedurally generated blockchain. During the bull market the viruses sleep — explore caverns and data-biomes for $DOGE, $SHIB, $PEPE, $BONK & rare $MOON shards, crack vaults for permanent protocols, and build automated cyber-defenses. Every bear market brings a different modifier and a specialized virus swarm. Choose exclusive rig modules, assemble all four rocket systems, then HODL through launch 🚀 to the moon.
