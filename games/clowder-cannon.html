DONT F*CK WITH CATS

Lead a swarm of cats through four levels of suspicious math gates. They auto-fire laser dots and hairballs at Roombas, cucumbers, bath water, comment-section hazards, and anything else that has chosen violence.

Bigger pack means more chaos. Zero cats means the run becomes one offended loaf.

Move with A/D, arrows, drag, or touch buttons. Cats shoot automatically because they rejected your management structure.

CAT MATH

  • GatesAdd kittens, double zoomies, or lose cats to cucumber panic.
  • BossesEach level ends with a louder household enemy and worse judgment.

INCIDENT LOG

How to play
  • Steer the cats. Drag or move left/right to choose a lane and pass through the better gate.
  • Let the cats fire. Larger groups fire faster. Hairballs hit hard, laser dots keep pressure on hazards.
  • Shoot bonus boxes. Cardboard boxes can add cats if destroyed before they pass.
  • Clear all four levels. Each boss gets tougher, and your surviving cats carry into the next zone.
