𝄞 CRESCENDO

The Conductor raised his baton and the world went silent. You are the Last Note, flying up the sheet music. Move with WASD — firing is automatic. Tap Space (or click) on the beat to crescendo your weapon up the power ladder; go quiet or miss the beat and the orchestra thins out. Headphones on. Play loud.

FIELD GUIDE

▲The Last Note — your ship. Auto-fires straight up; the white dot is your true hitbox.
◇Metronome bar — bottom center. Ticks converge on the beat; tap when they meet to crescendo.
▮Groove meter — left edge, SOLO up to TUTTI FORTISSIMO. It drains while you're quiet; keep tapping on beat.
⬡The Muted — silenced squadrons. They only fire on the beat; hear the rhythm, read the bullets.
𝄐Fermata — a screen-clearing bomb, earned with an 8-tap perfect streak. Spend it with B.
♛Section leaders — a boss ends each movement. Silence all four and the world sings again.
How to play
  • The soundtrack is the game. Every drum, bassline and arp is synthesized live, and it grows a layer each movement. Enemies fire on the beat — if you can hear it, you can dodge it.
  • Tap on the beat to power up. Each on-beat tap climbs the weapon ladder: SOLO → DUET → TRIO … all the way to TUTTI FORTISSIMO (seven-way spread, homing missiles, piercing center beam). Off-beat taps knock you down a rung.
  • Silence is decay. The groove meter drains a level every few seconds you don't tap. Great players play the game like a metronome — dodge with one hand, keep time with the other.
  • Fermata bombs. An 8-tap perfect streak banks a Fermata (max 2): a screen-clearing hold that erases every bullet, Raiden-style.
  • Keep the combo singing. Kills raise your multiplier up to ×5. Taking a hit resets it and drops two weapon levels — the score chase is a no-hit chase.
  • Four movements, four bosses. The Timpanist, the First Chair, the Metronome… and Him. Each movement is faster, higher, louder.
  • Encore. Fall once and a quick ad revives you with full health (Pro members take their encore free).
