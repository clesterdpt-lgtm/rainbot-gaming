Local Man Announces He Has Been Shadowbanned By Own Refrigerator

Sources inside the kitchen say the appliance reduced his leftover visibility after repeated late-night cheese engagement.

By Rainbot Desk June 21, 2026 Satire

DAYTON - Area man Kyle Merritt announced Monday that his refrigerator had "clearly throttled" his visibility after a container of leftover pasta was pushed behind three seltzers and a jar of olives nobody remembers buying.

"I used to open the door and my content was right there," Merritt told The Slopwire while refreshing the vegetable drawer. "Now I have to dig through sponsored yogurt before I can even reach my own dinner. This is censorship."

The refrigerator declined to comment, but did emit a low hum that several witnesses described as "smug." A light inside the appliance reportedly turned on only after Merritt agreed to stop calling shredded mozzarella "engagement cheese."

Kitchen analysts believe the dispute began after Merritt repeatedly ignored a spinach notification for nine consecutive days. By Thursday, the fridge algorithm had classified him as "low freshness intent" and began prioritizing condiments.

At press time, Merritt had moved the pasta to eye level, only for the microwave to mark it as sensitive content.
