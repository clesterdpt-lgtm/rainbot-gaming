THE ASCENT

Kenosis is a mountain, not a basin. You land at the Basecamp gate with the whole climb in one frame, and the Via Sacra spirals anticlockwise past seven stations to the Cathedral of the Ninth Ascent on the 452m summit.

This is an environment build. The mountain, its weather, its snow and its architecture are finished; the guardians are not here yet. Walk it, climb it, and look back down.

The nine stations
  • The Basecamp — south gate, 0m. Where you land.
  • The Black Tarn — SW, 40m. A lake of black ice and drowned pilgrims.
  • The Avalanche Bowl — SE, 60m. Open snowfield under a loaded headwall.
  • The Glacier Tongue — NW, 95m. Seracs, crevasses and three moulins.
  • The Rime Forest — E, 140m. Petrified conifers armoured in wind-driven rime.
  • The Fumarole Steps — NE, 160m. The mountain is not dead.
  • The Frozen Cascade — N, 210m. A 130m waterfall caught mid-fall.
  • The Bell Terrace — W, 240m. The Carillon Priory and its nine frozen bells.
  • The Cathedral of the Ninth Ascent — the summit, 452m.
How to play
  • Move with WASD, vault with Space, and click the playfield to capture the mouse.
  • With White Vigil selected, hold primary fire to alternate short-range crescent energy pulses between both hybrid blades.
  • Tap Shift to ignite the glide and hold it to keep gliding; hold Shift + Space for the jetpack.
  • Deep snow is slower than wind slab, and glacier ice will not hold an edge. The mountain decides your speed.
  • Press M for the ascent map. Press Tab or Esc for the field menu.
  • Crevasses are real holes. So is the drop off the Bell Terrace.
