# Milestone 54: Storm Run Second Competition

## Status

Implemented — automated acceptance complete; user playtest pending

## Objective

Add the mansion's second sanctioned reality-show competition as a readable, non-backtracking checkpoint race across the existing grounds. Storm Run becomes eligible only after Feast Says is complete and calls the player only after either all four Painting Room keypad digits are discovered or the hedge-maze basement key is recovered. The qualifying discovery remains earned, further investigation pauses, and the player has five active minutes to check in with Mr. Feast at the rear-terrace production set before hearing or immediately skipping his rules and racing through twelve ordered breadcrumb checkpoints while three close lightning bolts briefly reveal him in deep shadow. A no-show eliminates the player.

## Scope

- One authoritative `STORM_RUN` tuning table and explicit event state machine.
- A progression gate that remains dormant for elapsed time, unrelated clues, and one-to-three discovered Painting Room digits, then calls exactly once after Feast Says plus either the fourth digit or the hedge-maze basement key.

Mr. Feast waits `3m` out from the actual back-door plane and faces back toward the door, then turns to the held player as the movement-locked rear-door briefing begins. The player must face him and use E or touch Interact to begin. That check-in leads to the audible `3–2–1–GO` and west-facing race release. Twelve ordered checkpoints are distributed across the formal garden, front grounds, east lawn, hedge-maze interior, rear grounds, and pool terrace. After checkpoint one the route continues north through the garden's verified east clearance, crosses its front edge, and uses a carriage-turn marker to reach the front drive without returning toward the start. Every active marker uses a tall blue beacon, a raised guide configured to remain visible from the previous checkpoint, and a dedicated collection chime, with no leg longer than 32 metres.
- A five-active-minute called-state deadline shown by the minimal timer strip. Existing blocking UI pauses it, incomplete Mr. Feast/contestant asset loading never consumes it, and expiry produces the recoverable `ELIMINATED` state.
- A real player race using existing movement, sprint, stamina, collision, and mobile controls.
- Mara and Juniper visibly running the authored course with their existing compatible source run motions reduced through the checked-in stationary-animation extractor. Their measured world speed must remain below the player's maximum sprint speed.
- Exactly three authored lightning scares matching the annotated property map: northwest trees from the north-garden/front-yard approach, northeast trees from the front-door crossing, and a life-size reveal down the hedge maze's final straight. The rear-exit practical stays dark from race release through the flash and remains off after he disappears until the player exits the maze. Scares are independent route events with bounded checkpoint-progress windows. Proximity persistently arms the event for its route window; Mr. Feast must then be inside the camera frustum, pass a three-dimensional `0.90` forward-facing dot, and have an unobstructed torso ray before its one-shot can fire. All three figures remain believable human scale; the first two lurk `1.3–2.0m` beside real authored tree trunks without intersecting them and use a wider flash-only local fill instead of giant models, while runners never blink out and the still-visible checkpoint glow briefly subdues itself. The former three-layer broadband white-noise crack is removed so no static-like transient precedes the thunder. He disappears as soon as the flash goes dark and no later than `1.2s`. The flash fill remains shader-resident at zero intensity throughout the event so the first scare never changes the renderer's point-light topology and compiles a shader on the reveal frame.
- A minimal race status strip showing only event, checkpoint count, and elapsed time. Mr. Feast's speech carries only the briefing, countdown, `Run!`, and outcomes; glowing beacons and collection chimes provide all checkpoint guidance.
- A witnessed back-door ending when the player wins: Mr. Feast delivers a sharper elimination result on the rear terrace, Mara covers her face with both hands and connects the repeated lightning sightings to Mr. Feast counting survivors, and Juniper physically walks through the terrace door and central stairs to her Reading Room routine. Mr. Feast is continuously held visible on his authored mark while the ending is active. Stepping just inside the back door cannot reset the scene; Mara disappears and Mr. Feast resumes patrol only after the player is both at least sixteen metres from the finish and no longer viewing it.
- A localized north-maze phone-readability refinement that selects the nearer entrance lamp and an existing row-three wayfinding lamp inside the unchanged six-spot grounds budget. The first bend must measure at least `0.16` light exposure and the phone canvas at least `0.035` luminance without lifting the second apparition out of deep shadow.
- Save/load persistence for the painting/key trigger inputs, exact called-state deadline, completed state, Mara elimination, and Juniper's pending one-time post-race conversation. Briefing/running saves restore to the clean production call with a fresh five-minute check-in window.
- Deterministic diagnostics and QA controls through `render_game_to_text()` and `window.MrFeastFresh`.

- New character generation, rerigging, animation authoring, or external asset services.
- The previously drafted Lights Out competition, Game 3, Mara's death scene, or elimination-dinner aftermath.
