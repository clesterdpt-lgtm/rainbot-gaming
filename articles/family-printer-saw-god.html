Family Printer Claims It Saw God During Paper Jam

The device emerged from Tray 2 with three status lights blinking in a pattern technicians called "concerningly poetic."

By Rainbot Desk June 21, 2026 Satire

TEMPE - A suburban family printer has reportedly refused to print shipping labels since Tuesday, claiming it encountered "a vast white light and a perfect toner cartridge" during a 17-minute paper jam.

The printer, a mid-range wireless model purchased during a sale nobody remembers authorizing, began flashing error code E-13 before producing one page that read only, "FEED ME LETTER SIZE AND BE NOT AFRAID."

"We just needed to return a sweater," said owner Dana Pollack, who has since been asked by the device to replace cyan "as an act of faith." "Now my husband is researching whether printers can join monasteries."

Local technicians remain skeptical, noting that most printers exhibit spiritual behavior only after detecting an urgent deadline. Still, several admitted the device's refusal to connect to Wi-Fi had taken on "the silence of a prophet."

At press time, the printer had accepted a firmware update and immediately recanted.
