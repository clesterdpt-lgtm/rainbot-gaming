Mission Briefing
  • Steer Boat: Use A / D or ← / → (hold to steer into turns).
  • Throttle & Brake: Use W / ↑ to accelerate, S / ↓ to brake/reverse.
  • Horn & Flares: Press Spacebar or the HORN button to trigger acoustic shockwaves that clear mines and deflect incoming missiles.
  • Naval Contact Mines: Floating spiked mines bobbing in water. Direct hit causes hull explosion.
  • Anti-Ship Missiles: Fast coastal missiles. Watch for red laser targeting beams on the water before impact!
  • Terrorist Speedboats: Fast hostile skiffs that flank and fire tracer bursts. Dodge their fire or blast them with flares.
  • Oil Barrels (🛢️): Collect floating barrels to boost score and charge your Cargo Multiplier!
  • Repair Kits (🧰): Restores 1 lost Hull pip.
