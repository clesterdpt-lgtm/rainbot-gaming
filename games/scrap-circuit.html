HOW TO PLAY

  • Last chassis standing. You get three lives against five AI rivals.
  • Grab pickups. Machine guns, homing missiles, freeze shots, mines, ricochet bombs, shields, turbo.
  • Charge your special. Every vehicle has a signature move — it's the purple bar. Batteries charge it faster.
  • Break the scenery. Fences, barrels, snack stands, and tombstones pay Salvage. Explosive barrels pay in shrapnel.
  • Fear the arena. Car crushers, phantom freight, coaster trains, popping graves, and long falls are all "excluded perils."

THE ADJUSTER SAYS

PS1 notes
  • Rendered at a 270p internal target and upscaled with hard pixels, like a grey console in 1997.
  • Vertices snap to a coarse grid on purpose. The wobble is the point.
  • Fog is doing structural work. Each arena has its own flavor of "you can't see far, that's the vibe."
  • Chunky 64–128px texture tiles load from assets/textures/scrap-circuit/, with flat colors still available as a fallback.
