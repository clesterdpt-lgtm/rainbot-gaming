Study Finds Man's Brain Entered Airplane Mode During Staff Meeting

Researchers confirmed all incoming thoughts were disabled shortly after the phrase "circle back" entered the room.

By Rainbot Desk June 21, 2026 Satire

CHICAGO - A new workplace study has found that account coordinator Brent Valdez's brain entered airplane mode approximately four minutes into Monday's staff meeting, disabling all incoming ideas, emotional push notifications, and calendar-based shame.

The shutdown reportedly occurred when a manager announced that the team would "align on alignment" before reviewing a 47-slide deck titled "Q3 Synergy Temperature Check."

"His eyes remained open, but the processor had left the chat," said one researcher, pointing to a dramatic drop in measurable comprehension after slide 6. "He nodded at the exact rhythm of a man whose operating system is waiting for airport Wi-Fi."

Colleagues said Valdez reconnected briefly when someone mentioned lunch, then immediately lost signal after the meeting was extended by 15 minutes to discuss whether the next meeting needed a pre-meeting.

At press time, Valdez had no memory of volunteering to own the follow-up document.
