FIELD GUIDE

▲Requiem craft — four unlockable ships, each with its own hull, score, tempo, and weapon. The white dot is your true hitbox.
◇Metronome bar — bottom center. Ticks converge on the beat; tap when they meet to crescendo.
▮Groove meter — left edge, nine stages of the selected ship's signature weapon. It drains while you're quiet; keep tapping on beat.
✦Cadenza — land 4 PERFECT taps in a row for four beats of faster, harder fire and a local bullet clear.
◎Graze — skim enemy bullets with your ship's wings for bonus score and a little more time before your Groove decays.
⬡The Muted — silenced squadrons. They only fire on the beat; hear the rhythm, read the bullets.
𝄐Fermata — a screen-clearing bomb, earned with an 8-tap on-beat streak. Spend it with B.
♛Section leaders — a boss ends each movement. Silence all four and the world sings again.
AGamepad — left stick or D-pad moves, A taps, B/RB drops a Fermata, and Start pauses.
How to play
  • The soundtrack is the game. Every drum, bassline and arp is synthesized live, and it grows a layer each movement. Enemies fire on the beat — if you can hear it, you can dodge it.
  • Choose your instrument. Aria's Harmonic Array fills the field, Vesper's Prism Lance pierces a narrow lane, Counterpoint's Canon Drones weave together, and Virtuoso's Baton Rail accents every beat. Their tempos and live arrangements are different too.
  • Tap on the beat to power up. Each on-beat tap climbs the selected ship's nine-stage weapon ladder. Off-beat taps knock you down a rung; every craft develops in a different way.
  • Play the center of the beat. The cyan zone is good; the narrow gold zone is PERFECT. Chain four PERFECT taps to trigger a four-beat Cadenza: faster fire, extra damage, and a close-range bullet clear.
  • Graze the danger. Your white center dot is the true hitbox. Let bullets pass through the wings to earn graze score and delay Groove decay.
  • Silence is decay. The groove meter drains a level every few seconds you don't tap. Great players play the game like a metronome — dodge with one hand, keep time with the other.
  • Fermata bombs. An 8-tap on-beat streak banks a Fermata (max 2): a screen-clearing hold that erases every bullet, Raiden-style.
  • Keep the combo singing. Kills raise your multiplier up to ×5. Taking a hit resets it and drops two weapon levels — the score chase is a no-hit chase.
  • Four movements, four bosses. The Timpanist, the First Chair, the Metronome… and Him. Each movement is faster, higher, louder.
  • Earn the ovation. Every section ends with a D-to-S grade and score bonus based on beat accuracy, PERFECT timing, grazes, and taking no damage.
  • Controller ready. Left stick/D-pad moves, A taps, B or RB bombs, and Start pauses. Rhythm actions trigger once per press, never from holding a button.
  • Encore. Fall once and a quick ad revives you with full health (Pro members take their encore free).
