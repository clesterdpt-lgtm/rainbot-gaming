      How to play
      • Roll on your turn. Buy unowned property, pay rent to rivals, and collect $200 when you pass Start.
      • Renovate owned property. Landing on your own deed lets you raise its rent up to level 3.
      • Watch market heat. Hot markets raise rent and net worth, but special cards can cool the room fast.
      • Win after 16 rounds by having the highest net worth. Going negative bankrupts you.
      • Power-ups (ad): HELOC Cash, Zoning Variance, and Rent Shield.
