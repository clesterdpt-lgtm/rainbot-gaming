🎯 DRONE HUNTER

The sky is full of drones — delivery bots, surveillance cams, and rogue ad-drones. Aim and shoot to bag them before they buzz off. Hit your quota each round to advance.

But the 🕊️ peace doves are protected — plug one and the robo-dog files a report.

Move the crosshair with your mouse, click to fire (or just tap on mobile). P pauses. 3 shots per wave.

CONTROLS

TARGETS

📦Delivery drone — the bread and butter. +100.
👁️Surveillance cam — fast and jittery. +250.
👑Mega ad-drone — rare jackpot. +500.
🎁Supply crate — shoot for a random power-up: ammo, EMP, or slow-mo.
🛸Prime Mothership — boss every 3rd round. Multi-hit, big bonus.
🕊️Peace dove — PROTECTED. Three doves down = license revoked.
How to play
  • Snipe the drones. Each wave releases one to three drones while protected doves cut across the sightline. You get 3 shots — make them count before the drones escape off the top.
  • Hit your quota. Bag enough drones each round to advance. Fall short and it's game over.
  • Spare the doves. The 🕊️ peace doves are protected airspace. Each one you shoot is a strike — three strikes and your license is revoked.
  • Build a combo. Consecutive hits stack a multiplier. A wasted shot or a dove resets it. Clear a whole wave with no misses for a PERFECT bonus and an extra bullet.
  • Grab supply crates. Shoot a 🎁 crate for a random power-up — ammo refill, a screen-clearing EMP, or slow-motion.
  • Survive the boss & bonus rounds. A Prime Mothership shows up every 3rd round, and every 4th round is a no-penalty frenzy. Mind the dog — it fetches your kills and laughs when one gets away.
