THE DEADLINE HOUSE

Nobody's home. The power still works, mostly. Wander the basement, the main floor, and the bedrooms upstairs while the storm keeps knocking.

Click to look around · WASD to move · Shift to run · E to open doors, cabinets, the fridge, and light switches.

CONTROLS

WASD move · click game to look
Esc release mouse · Shift run · E interact

THE HOUSE

Basement — unfinished, dark. Pull the string for the bulb.
Main floor — living room, dining room, kitchen (open the fridge), and a bathroom.
Upstairs — three bedrooms and a full bath. Doors, cabinets, the fridge, and light switches are interactive.

About

A fictional spooky house sandbox — an atmospheric 3D environment with no scores and no chase. Not affiliated with any real person, brand, or location.
