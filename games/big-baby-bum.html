Big Baby Bum

A baby wakes up in a fenced backyard on the edge of a whole city — and the fence only opens one way: eat it. Eat everything smaller than you. Grow rounder until you become a ball and ROLL — through yards, streets, traffic, and eventually entire buildings. Do not eat the broccoli. Puking makes you shrink.

Controls
  • Move with WASD or arrow keys (camera-relative) — or hold the mouse / your finger on the ground where you want to go.
  • Space (or the 💨 button) fires a Turbo Fart: speed burst, vacuums nearby snacks, and leaves a stink-gas trail that stuns pursuers.
  • P pauses. M mutes.
  • Avoid anything with green stink lines (broccoli, dirty diapers, porta-potties, garbage trucks…) — puking shrinks you and slows you down for a moment. Hazards far smaller than you get squashed harmlessly, and their warnings switch off.
  • Around 1 m the baby goes full sphere and starts rolling with real momentum. Plan your braking distance near hazards — including moving traffic.
  • Once you leave the yard, mixed hunter packs come for you — guard dogs, territorial geese, furious babysitters, runaway mowers, police cruisers, diaper drones, HOA tanks, and demolition dozers. Melee hits and dodgeable air support shrink you a little. Break away, gas them, or outgrow them for a revenge snack.
  • The city never changes: buildings and trees you eat stay eaten in your save, while snacks, toys, people, and cars keep respawning.
