How to play
  • It's all about %. You don't have a health bar — you have a damage percent. The higher it climbs, the farther you fly when hit. Knock a rival off the screen (past the blast zone) to take a stock.
  • Stocks = lives. Pick 2, 3 or 5 stocks. Lose them all and you're out. Last fighter standing wins the match.
  • Attacks vs. specials. J does tilts, aerials and — with a quick direction flick — launching smash attacks. K is your character's special: tap it plus a direction for neutral / side / up (recovery) / down specials.
  • Recover to the ledge. Double-jump and use your up-special to get back. Falling near the stage edge grabs the ledge automatically — press up or toward the stage to climb.
  • Defend. Hold L to shield. Shield + a direction flick rolls or spot-dodges. I grabs through shields — then flick a direction to throw.
  • Arcade Run. Pick Arcade from the menu for a 5-fight ladder with rotating stages, rising CPU skill, a boss finale, run scoring and a grade.
  • Watch the stage. Every level has a hazard — ad-drone fly-bys, oil slicks & missile strikes, an express train, a bounce fountain and a falling chandelier. Use them on your rivals, or get launched yourself.
