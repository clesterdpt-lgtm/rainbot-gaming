THE GREEN ANTIPHON

Standing by

THE SURVEY

Meridian-IV is an ocean. Ninety per cent water, a scatter of volcanic atolls, and on one of them the reliquary hauler Antiphon came down forty years ago and broke into three pieces across the reef.

You land on the black sand at the south gate with the whole level in one frame: the lagoon, the ship's spine crossing it, the drive section standing in the pass, and the Cauldron on your left.

This is an environment build. The atoll, its sea, its weather, its jungle and the wreck are finished; the guardians are not here yet. Walk the ring, cross the lagoon, and climb the plug.

The nine places
  • The Landing — S, 4m. Black volcanic sand. Where you arrive.
  • The Prow — SE. Ninety metres of bow driven into the reef at 28°.
  • The Drowned Nave — E. A mangrove maze in knee-deep tea-coloured water.
  • The Bone Reef — NE. Bleached coral at low tide. The brightest place here.
  • The Drive Cathedral — N. The reactor section, half-sunk in the pass.
  • The Weeping Steps — NW, 74m. A waterfall down hexagonal basalt.
  • The Canopy Roost — W, 62m. A platform in the emergent crowns.
  • The Cauldron — SW, 214m. The volcanic plug. The island's peak.
  • The Reliquary Hold — centre, 34m. Mid-spine, open to the sky.
How to play
  • Move with WASD, vault with Space, and click the playfield to capture the mouse.
  • Tap Shift to ignite the glide and hold it to keep gliding; hold Shift + Space for the jetpack.
  • Wading is slower than walking. Past thigh depth the current will push you back — the lagoon is eight metres deep and you cannot swim in that armour.
  • The Spine is how you cross the lagoon on foot. Find where it comes ashore.
  • Wet sand, dry sand, coral rubble and jungle loam all hold an edge differently.
  • Press M for the survey map. Press Tab or Esc for the field menu.
