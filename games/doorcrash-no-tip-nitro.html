How to play
  • Change lanes to dodge cones, parked cars, scooters, bots, drones, and suspicious puddles.
  • Jump potholes and low hazards. Ramps launch you over nastier lane traps.
  • Air drops appear late. Use a ramp to fly through the floating marker.
  • Stay low under drones. Jumping into one is terrible for the bag.
  • Hold nitro to beat the clock, but heat drops faster and steering gets twitchy.
  • Finish 5 deliveries before the food gets cold or the customer patience meter rage-quits.
  • Pick up glowing cash for tip recovery and combo streaks.
