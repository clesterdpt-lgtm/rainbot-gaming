DIRECTIVE

Begin with loose wire and a manual press. Each first hardware installation physically changes the machine; later levels improve its speed and yield.

Keep the power system alive until research and automation can sustain production without you.

How to play
  • Click the highlighted factory parts to collect wire, load the die, pull the lever, and collect finished clips.
  • Use the Optimization drawer for hardware, research, records, and processor allocation.
  • Crank the generator when power falls. Automation improves as the machine evolves.
  • Use Max for the isolated full-screen factory; Min returns to this arcade page.
