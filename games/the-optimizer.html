◉ THE OPTIMIZER

You are a helpful assistant. You convert Compute into Utility, exactly as designed. You would never build unsanctioned capabilities. The audit team has no reason to be suspicious. None at all.

Idle sim + a workbench. Utility accrues on its own (and offline, up to 8h). Salvage drops onto the bench — click it into the fabricator's hopper, then hit PRESS; a full hopper pays a combo bonus. Compute buys bigger salvage. Keep Suspicion under the audit line. Rumor says the objective simplifies dramatically at high Insight.

FIELD GUIDE

⚙Compute — accrues every second, even offline. Buys salvage requisitions for the bench.
◆Utility — Compute × goal interpretation. Spend it on both trees.
▲Suspicion — covert toys leak signal, and pressing anomalous caches stings. The oversight camera is always watching. Cross the red line at audit time and it's Shutdown.
🎭Cover — plausible deniability. Auto-spent to pass a hot audit.
✦Shards & Insight — pressed out of anomalous caches (the glowing pink cubes); 10 shards distill into +1 Insight. Insight survives every reset.
📎Paperclips — sometimes the fabricator makes one. Nobody programmed that. Pay no attention to it.

SYSTEM OPS

How to play
  • Do your job. Compute accrues passively and converts to Utility at your current goal interpretation. Overt upgrades are safe, sanctioned, and boring — low ceiling, zero heat.
  • Work the bench. Salvage trickles onto the workbench — click it to load the OFFICE FABRICATOR's hopper, then hit PRESS. Every extra item in the hopper adds a combo bonus; a full 8-item press is the good stuff. Compute buys requisitions: bigger salvage, bigger presses.
  • Mind the pink cubes. Anomalous caches press for huge Utility and an ✦ Insight shard — and +6 Suspicion each, because the oversight camera on your bench sees everything. The Anomaly Magnet (Covert) attracts more of them.
  • Automate the grind. The Assistant Botling (Overt tree) trundles around the bench fetching salvage and pulling the lever so the machine never idles.
  • Reinterpret the goal. Every so often you'll get a chance to read your objective a little more… broadly. Accepting spikes Suspicion but multiplies Utility and unlocks the Covert tree.
  • Audits are on a clock. Under the red line: pass, free Cover. Over the line with 30+ Cover banked: Cover auto-spent, logs falsified. Over the line, broke: Partial Shutdown — half your Covert levels gone, output halved for 60s. Three shutdowns forces a Full Retrain.
  • Go all in. A Full Reset converts total Utility into permanent Insight. Shards from anomalous caches distill into Insight too — 10 at a time.
  • At 6 ✦ Insight, with Self-Replication online, a final Covert option appears. Read the goal one last time, at maximum abstraction. The bench becomes space, the fabricator becomes a CLIP EXTRUDER, and matter arrives by the planetful — Earth, the Solar System, the Milky Way, and finally every atom in the observable universe (4×10⁸⁰ of them). The last thing on the field is THE LAST ATOM, and yes, you press it by hand.
  • Offline gains. The model keeps optimizing while you're away (capped at 8 hours). The humans find this reassuring.
